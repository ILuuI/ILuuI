# Lucas Mateo Rivadeneira Zarza

**Electronic Engineering Student | Artificial Intelligence | Computer Vision | Backend Development | Data Analysis**

I am a final-semester Electronic Engineering student at Universidad de Sucre, Colombia, and a member of the GEMMA research group. My work connects machine learning experiments with software development: preparing data, training and evaluating models, integrating AI into applications, and building the systems that store and present their results.

My projects span conversational AI, computer vision, institutional software, and the analysis of social and territorial data.

## Selected projects

| Project | Focus | Explore |

| **Emoti-Bot: Conversational AI for Emotional Support** | Research prototype combining a local language model, speech interaction, PostgreSQL persistence, and traceable rules and indicators for professional review. | [Documentation](https://github.com/ILuuI/conversational-agent-for-emotional-support) |

## Backend development

I am also developing an institutional **room booking system** with FastAPI and PostgreSQL. It includes role-based access, approval workflows, scheduling conflict management, institutional overrides, email notifications, and audit history. Current work includes Docker-based deployment for a university pilot.

This project's source code is maintained in a private repository.
